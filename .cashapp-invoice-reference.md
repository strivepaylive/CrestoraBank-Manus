# Cash App Invoice/Receipt Layout Reference

## Style 1 - Receipt Style (index 6):
- Cash App logo (black rounded square with white $) centered at top
- "Cash App" text centered below logo
- Date/time below (e.g., "11/15/2024, 2:34:18 PM")
- Dashed separator line
- Transaction Type: "Payment Sent" (right-aligned)
- From: $YourCashTag (right-aligned)
- To: $JohnSmith (right-aligned)
- Dashed separator
- Note: "Dinner split" (left-aligned description)
- Amount Sent: $45.00 (right-aligned)
- Fee: $0.00 (right-aligned)
- Total: $45.00 (right-aligned)
- === separator
- Payment Method: Cash App Balance (right-aligned)
- New Balance: $127.45 (right-aligned)
- --- separator
- "Thank you for using Cash App!" (centered, green text)
- "Questions? Visit cash.app/help" (centered)
- "or email support@cash.app" (centered)
- === separator
- QR code at bottom

## Style 2 - Payment Confirmation (index 7):
- Avatar circle with initial letter (colored background)
- Name bold centered below avatar
- "Payment to $$CashTag" in gray below name
- Large amount in light gray/green ($100)
- Date below amount
- Green checkmark + "Received" status
- Details section:
  - Amount: $100
  - Source: Bank of American
  - Identifier: #180HFOK
  - To: Michael Sinclair
  - From: Sarah Sophia
- Footer: Block Inc., 1455 Market St, Suite 600, San Francisco, CA 94103, (855) 351-2274

## Colors:
- Cash App Green: #00D632 (bright green)
- Background: White
- Text: Black/Dark gray
- Secondary text: #9CA3AF (gray)
- Logo background: Black with white $ sign

## For our invoice we will combine both styles:
- Cash App green logo at top
- Sender name + cashtag
- Avatar icon with initial
- Amount prominently displayed
- Description/Note
- Fee breakdown (Amount, Fee, Total)
- Remaining Balance
- Custom message/notes
- QR code or barcode
- Footer with Cash App branding
- Sender email: CashApp-border@crestorabank.com
